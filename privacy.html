Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Niblr is an app for mapping wild edible plants. This policy explains what it collects, why, and who else sees it. It is written to describe what the app actually does — not to reserve rights we don't use.

The short version We store your account details, what you post, and the location of each find you pin. Pinned locations are public by design — that is the point of a shared map. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Deleting your account erases your data.

Who we are

Niblr is an independent app. For anything in this policy, including access and deletion requests, contact rvdesign12089@gmail.com.

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address and a password, handled by our authentication provider. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; we never see them.
  • Username, display name, optional bio, optional profile photo.

What you post

  • Photos of your finds.
  • Species name, category, and any field notes you write.
  • The coordinates and place name of each find you pin.
  • Observations you add to other people's finds: a quality rating, fruiting status, and the date you saw it.
  • Likes, saved finds, verification votes, reports, and blocks.

Device location

If you grant location permission, your device's position is used while the app is open to centre the map, sort finds by distance, and suggest a place name when you pin something. Your live position is not stored on our servers and is not shared with other users. The coordinate you choose for a find is stored, and is visible to everyone.

Diagnostics

To find and fix crashes, the app records error messages and a small number of usage events (for example, "signed up", "logged a find") along with your account id, the platform, and the app version. There is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK in the app.

What is public

Your username, display name, bio, profile photo, and every find you post — including its location, photo and notes — are visible to anyone using the app. Your email address is never shown to other users. Consider carefully before pinning a spot on private land or one you would rather keep to yourself.

Who else processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication and photo storage. Data is stored on their infrastructure in the United States.
  • Apple — distributes the app, including through TestFlight during the beta.
  • OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors — supply map tiles. Requesting a tile reveals your IP address and the area of the map you are looking at to the tile provider.
  • Place-name lookup — when you pin a find, the coordinate is sent to a reverse-geocoding service to turn it into a readable place name. On iPhone and Android this is the operating system's own geocoder; on the web it is BigDataCloud.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Your account and posts are kept until you delete them. Diagnostic records are kept while they are useful for debugging and are not linked to anything beyond your account id.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to your profile, and choose to delete your account. This permanently removes your profile, your finds, your photos and your activity. It cannot be undone. If you cannot reach the app, email rvdesign12089@gmail.com and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it. Email rvdesign12089@gmail.com and we will respond as quickly as we can. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Niblr is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in any way that matters, we will update the date at the top and prompt you in the app before you continue using it.
