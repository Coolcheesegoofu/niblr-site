Installing it

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download Niblr for Android.
  2. When the download finishes, tap Open (or find niblr.apk in your Downloads).
  3. If your phone says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the developer, tap More details → Install anyway. That warning appears for every app installed from outside the Play Store.
  5. Open Niblr, create an account, and confirm your email.
Updates Most fixes arrive by themselves the next time you open the app. If a bigger update needs a new download, it will be on this page.
Foraging safety Identifications in Niblr are never a guarantee that something is safe to eat or touch. Deadly look-alikes exist for many edible species. Never eat anything based on this app alone — confirm with a qualified local expert.
