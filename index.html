Niblr

An app for finding, mapping and logging wild edible plants.

Drop a pin where something grows, and it joins a map everyone can see. Other people can verify it, rate how good the patch was, and note when it was last fruiting. Niblr covers plants only — never fungi.

Foraging safety Identifications in Niblr are never a guarantee that something is safe to eat or touch. Deadly look-alikes exist for many edible species. Never eat anything based on this app alone — confirm with a qualified local expert.
