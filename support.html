Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile, then choose to delete your account. It permanently removes your profile, finds, photos and activity, and cannot be undone. If you can't reach the app, email us and we will do it for you.

Someone posted something they shouldn't have

Tap the flag on the find or comment. You can also block the person, which hides everything they post from you immediately.

Is it safe to eat what I find here?

Not on the strength of this app. Identifications, including community verifications, are never a safety guarantee, and deadly look-alikes exist for many edible species. Always confirm with a qualified local expert before eating anything.

Why does a pin need my location?

Only to centre the map on you, sort finds by distance, and suggest a place name when you pin something. Your live position is never stored or shared — see the Privacy Policy.

I didn't get my confirmation email

Check spam first. If it still hasn't arrived, email us the address you signed up with and we'll sort it out.
